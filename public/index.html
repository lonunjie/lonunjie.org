Why we exist

What is the purpose of life?

We can look close and care for our families. It helps preserve our species.

Or look at the big picture. Earth is the only space rock known to host life, and with a superabundance of it. Sadly, humans are accelerating the extinction of species on our planet. We want to protect the diversity of life in the only place we know it exists.

Think bigger. In the future, will we be in the position to seed life across the universe? This will require scientific discoveries and technological advances. We will need to cooperate, to build a future for life to bloom.

It’s a big task. Let’s do what we can. Let’s plan for the future of life.
